Tips for best results

  • Show one hand, well lit, filling about a third of the frame.
  • Keep your whole hand visible and hold still for a moment.
  • A letter is added once. Lower your hand before repeating it.
  • Use Delete to correct mistakes and Space between words.

About Sign to Speech

What it does. This recognises static ASL fingerspelling: 24 letters, with J and Z excluded because they need motion. Hold one handshape steadily to add a letter, then build text and hear it spoken.

What it does not do. It is not a sign language translator. It does not understand ASL grammar, facial expression, continuous signing, or other sign languages.

Demo model. Predictions use synthetic training data and are for demonstration only. They are not reliable ASL recognition.

Recognition happens on this device. Video is not uploaded or saved.
