## Datasets and images

This repository does not include a training image dataset and does not
redistribute third-party sign-language photographs. The synthetic demo rows in
`data/demo/` are generated by `src/demo_data.py`; they are not real ASL data.

If you use an external dataset (for example a public ASL alphabet image set),
check its license and terms before use, keep images out of version control, and
do not redistribute them with this project.
